About me

Analyst mindset, built on operational reality

My path into IT didn't start behind a desk, it started on a warehouse floor and a retail counter, which is exactly why my analysis work stays grounded in how a business actually runs.

I'm a final-year Bachelor of IT in Business Systems student with a clear focus: business and data analysis. I like sitting with a process until I can draw it where it slows down, where information gets lost, where a small fix removes a daily headache. That's the work I trained for through requirements gathering, BPMN process mapping, SWOT and gap analysis, SQL, Excel and Power BI.

Before the analysis training, I spent two years in stock, warehouse and customer-facing roles managing inventory, processing transactions, and resolving issues face-to-face. That background is my second strength: I'm just as capable stepping into technical and field support work, comfortable with hands-on, on-site responsibility, and quick to pick up new tools and certifications.

What ties both together is reliability with detail whether that's a stock count that has to balance or a dataset that has to be right before a decision gets made on it.
